# Case Study: HarborPoint Health Services (Fictional)

**Status:** Simulated educational case study. No real organization, client, assessment, or confidential data is involved.

## Organization
HarborPoint Health Services is a hypothetical regional healthcare-services company with 420 employees, three clinics, a central billing office, and a small IT team. It relies on a cloud-hosted patient scheduling application, an electronic health record (EHR) vendor, Microsoft 365 email, remote-access VPN, and third-party billing services.

## Business priorities
1. Maintain patient-care scheduling and clinical operations.
2. Protect patient and employee information.
3. Maintain billing continuity and contractual trust.
4. Reduce disruption from ransomware and account compromise.

## Assumed security conditions
These are **fictional assumptions**, not discovered vulnerabilities:
- Multifactor authentication (MFA) is required for administrators but not all remote-access and email users.
- Backups exist, but restoration testing is inconsistent.
- Third-party vendor reviews occur at onboarding but are not refreshed annually.
- Endpoint patching is tracked manually, with some overdue devices.
- Staff receive annual security training, but phishing simulations and reporting exercises are infrequent.
- Centralized logging exists for a subset of critical systems.

## Assessment scope
In scope: identity and access management, backup resilience, vendor risk, endpoint patching, staff awareness, and detection/response readiness.

Out of scope: penetration testing, exploitation, clinical safety assessment, legal compliance certification, and verification of actual vendor controls.

## Method
Use a qualitative 1–5 likelihood and impact model, calculate a relative priority score, map recommended safeguards to NIST CSF 2.0 functions and outcomes, and produce an executive-facing remediation plan. All ratings are illustrative judgments based on the assumptions above.

## Limitations
No interviews, asset inventory, security logs, vulnerability scans, or control evidence were collected. This is a demonstration of a risk-assessment workflow, **not** a validated security assessment or compliance opinion.
