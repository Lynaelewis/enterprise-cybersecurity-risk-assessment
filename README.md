# Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**A fictional cybersecurity consulting case study | NIST CSF 2.0 | Enterprise risk prioritization**

This portfolio project demonstrates how a cybersecurity analyst or consultant could translate technical control gaps into business risk, recommend proportionate safeguards, and communicate priorities to leadership.

> **Educational simulation:** HarborPoint Health Services is fictional. The scenario, vulnerabilities, scores, and recommendations are invented for learning purposes. This is not a real audit, client engagement, or compliance assessment.

## Scenario

HarborPoint Health Services is a hypothetical regional healthcare-services organization with 420 employees and several critical business systems. Leadership wants to understand which cybersecurity issues deserve attention over the next 90 days.

The case study evaluates six illustrative risks involving identity security, ransomware recovery, third-party vendors, endpoint patching, phishing, and security monitoring.

## Approach

- Identify business-critical services and assumed control gaps.
- Describe plausible threat events and business consequences.
- Rate likelihood and impact on a 1–5 scale.
- Prioritize risks using a transparent scoring rubric.
- Map proposed improvements to NIST CSF 2.0 functions and outcomes.
- Recommend accountable owners, target windows, and validation evidence.

## Skills demonstrated

Cybersecurity risk assessment · Security governance · NIST CSF 2.0 · Risk register development · Executive communication · Remediation prioritization · Vendor risk awareness

## Important limitations

This is a **document-based portfolio case study** with no real-world testing, scans, interviews, or collected evidence. The hypothetical organization is not a representation of a real company. Recommendations are illustrative and do not establish compliance.
